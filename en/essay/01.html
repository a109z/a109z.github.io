Reading the Corporation by Its Core Business and Core Competency

Weigh the present and the future. A journey to find two faces.

2026-11-12

One Corporation, Two Faces

A corporation has two faces.

One is the entity, the real thing. It does business under today's management conditions. Market, customers, assets, processes and rules belong here. They can be seen and measured.

The other is the legal person, the subject. It decides for itself and evolves for itself. It seeks to survive. It seeks to prosper.

The two faces do not stand apart. One corporation holds both.

Core Competency

The core competency is the ultimate competitive strength a company holds. It is the difference that sets it apart from others.

Core Business

The core business is the revenue and profit created by the core competency.

What share of total revenue does the core business hold? The corporation must know this clearly. This share is the present face of the entity.

The Journey

The face ahead is held in the vision and the future strategy. It is the face the corporation draws as a subject.

The corporation does business for that face. This is the journey.

Divining

To divine here does not mean to be right. It means to keep projecting how the core business, built on the core competency, will change in the future.

Reflect on the past record and the present competitors. As a subject, keep projecting the corporation's future, measure it against them, and move on.

In this way the plan and the vision are corrected. The corporation, as a subject, pursues lasting growth.

Digesting the Contradiction

In this process, the entity and the subject may clash, each with its own position and view. The entity speaks of today's numbers. The subject speaks of the vision ahead.

The corporation must digest this contradiction and develop further. That is the corporation as a developing subject.
